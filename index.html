Learning to restore, understand,

I am an M.S. student in Data Science at National Cheng Kung University, advised by Assoc. Prof. Chih-Chung Hsu. My research spans computer vision, image restoration, remote sensing imagery, and vision-language learning.

My master’s research focuses on diffusion-based defocus deblurring and controllable single-image refocusing. I build and evaluate deep learning models with Python and PyTorch.
